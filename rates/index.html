What can break the expression

DV01 neutrality is local, not risk neutrality. Curve flattening, convexity, changing hedge ratios and funding can create losses.

Read the hedge and the missing costs

A DV01-neutral 2s10s steepener pairs a long 2-year leg with a short 10-year leg, scaled to offset initial parallel-curve DV01. It seeks a widening of the 10-year minus 2-year yield spread. Parallel neutrality is local: it does not remove key-rate, convexity or large-move risk, and not every steepening path produces the same P&L.

Short exposure loses when bond prices rise and adds borrow, recall and margin risk. The scenarios omit financing, repo and borrow costs, coupon carry over a holding period, transaction costs and bid–ask spreads. The available snapshot contains no observed security prices or executable quotes.
