13-factor attribution

Markets, styles, rates and diversifiers

Source-estimated traded-spread factors; not Fama–French five-factor. Betas use 2,194 daily observations from 2018-01-01. The beta estimation window is distinct from the contribution period above.

Residual is not manager alpha

-1.273 pp is unexplained by the published factors. It may reflect omitted exposures, changing weights, estimation error or selection. It is not proof of persistent skill.

The source’s fit is an ex-post diagnostic, not an out-of-sample alpha test.

Exposure is not contribution or holding weight

A beta of 0.50 to equity-over-cash means a +1 pp move in that spread corresponds to about +0.50 pp of modeled fund return, with other factors fixed. It is not a 50% position weight.

Factor contribution depends on the factor’s path and the source attribution method. A large exposure can contribute little if its factor barely moves.
